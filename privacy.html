Privacy Policy — VOLTA

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how VOLTA (“the App,” “we,” “us”) handles information when you use the Android application VOLTA (package name: com.volta.volta).

1. Summary

VOLTA is designed to keep your game progress on your device. We do not operate user accounts, and this build does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. Optional local notifications may be used if you opt in.

2. Information we store on your device

  • Game save data (progress, settings, preferences), stored locally using on-device storage (for example SharedPreferences).
  • Your content-intensity and notification preferences.

This data stays on your device unless you back it up yourself (for example via your device's backup features) or share it somehow outside the App.

3. Notifications

If you opt in, the App may schedule local “dawn unlock” notifications so you know when the next in-game day is available. On Android this may use notification permission and boot-completed handling so a scheduled ping can be restored after reboot. These notifications are generated on your device; we do not send push messages from our servers.

4. Information we do not collect

In this build we do not intentionally collect:

  • Names, emails, or account credentials for VOLTA
  • Precise location
  • Contacts, photos, or microphone/camera data
  • Advertising IDs for our own ads (we do not run ads in this build)
  • Analytics events to a first-party or third-party analytics product we control

Google Play and your device manufacturer may process limited technical data as part of distributing or running apps (installs, crashes reported through Play, etc.). That processing is governed by Google's and your device maker's policies, not by us beyond what Play requires of developers.

5. Internet access

The App's Android package does not declare a general Internet permission for core gameplay. Store listing assets and this website are separate from in-app online accounts (there are none).

6. Children

VOLTA is intended only for adults 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used the App, contact us and we will help you delete local data by guiding you to uninstall or clear app data.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because saves live on your device, you control retention. Uninstalling the App or clearing the App's storage deletes local game data on that device. We do not maintain a separate cloud copy of your saves.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the App's data practices change (for example if we add accounts, cloud saves, or analytics). The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy for future use.
