About

VOLTA is a text-first cyberpunk RPG built around a nightly contract: fight on the Grid, talk to whoever is out, sleep in the coffin stacks, and wake to headlines about what moved while you were gone. Handles are street names. Classes break ICE differently. The writing is adult — violence, drugs, and sex in prose, not porn art.

Features

The Nightly Loop

Hub, Grid, Circuit, Chrome, Hunt, Civic and Stacks — one cycle, then the Ledger.

Three Class Arcs

Samurai, Netrunner and Ghost each cut through the city a different way.

Parallel Contracts

Consequence-driven choices that keep moving whether you're watching or not.

Local Saves

Progress lives on your device. No accounts, no cloud, no sign-in.

Dawn Unlock Pings

Optional, opt-in local notifications when the next in-game day is ready.
